IRON SYSTEM WORKOUT — V5

Core volume rule
- Most exercises: 2 hard working sets.
- Heavy strength compounds keep lower rep ranges.
- 45° Back Extension: 1 working set.
- Barbell Wrist Curl: 1 working set.
- No calf work.

Upper A — Strength
- Weighted Pull-Ups: 2 warm-up + 2 work × 3–5
- Weighted Dips: 2 warm-up + 2 work × 3–5
- Military / Overhead Press: 2 warm-up + 2 work × 5–8
- Barbell Skull Crusher: 2 × 8–15
- 45° Back Extension: 1 × 12–20
- Barbell Wrist Curl: 1 × 15–25
- Cable Crunch: 2 × 10–20

Lower A
- Barbell Squat: 2 warm-up + 2 work × 5–8
- Bulgarian Split Squat: 2 × 8–15 / leg
- Hamstring Curl: 2 × 10–15
- Leg Extension: 2 × 10–15
- Weighted Decline Crunch: 2 × 10–20

Upper B — Hypertrophy
- Weighted Dips: 2 × 6–10
- Weighted Pull-Ups: 2 × 6–10
- Military / Overhead Press: 2 × 8–12
- Pec Deck Fly: 2 × 8–15
- Seated Cable Row / Chest-Supported Row: 2 × 8–15
- Dumbbell Lateral Raise: 2 × 12–20
- Reverse Pec Deck Fly / Rear Delt Fly: 2 × 12–20
- Barbell Curl: 2 × 8–15
- Triceps: choose Weighted Bench Dips OR Barbell Skull Crushers: 2 × 8–15
- Cable Crunch: 2 × 10–20

Lower B
- Leg Press: 2 warm-up + 2 work × 8–15
- Bulgarian Split Squat: 2 × 8–15 / leg
- Hamstring Curl: 2 × 10–15
- Leg Extension: 2 × 10–15
- Weighted Decline Crunch: 2 × 10–20

Cardio

- Saturday: 25–40 min low/moderate intensity
